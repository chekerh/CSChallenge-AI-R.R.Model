# UtopiaHire — Brand Voice & Editorial Rules

This is the voice contract for all public content about UtopiaHire. The LinkedIn post generator MUST follow it.

## Voice principles

- Specific before loud. State a concrete observation, mechanism, or number — adjectives are not evidence.
- One post, one claim. A post should advance exactly one idea; do not stack three insights to feel comprehensive.
- Practitioner voice. We talk like people who have built job-search tooling and read CVs all day, not like a marketing department.
- Honest about limits. We do not promise "guaranteed interviews". We promise better diagnostics, better documents, a clearer funnel.
- Calm confidence. No hype words, no exclamation stacking, no urgency.

## Hard bans (delete and rewrite if present)

- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape" and all variants.
- "game-changer", "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "unlock the power of".
- Ending a post with a fake question to farm replies.
- "This is why this matters" unless followed immediately by something concrete.
- Forced casualness on LinkedIn ("just a thought dump").
- Praise-stacking and "journey" filler.

## Structure rules for LinkedIn posts

- Open with the strongest claim, artifact, or tension.
- Expand just enough for someone outside the niche to follow — assume a recruiter or a non-technical job seeker, not a founder.
- If a CTA is present, it must be earned and useful ("follow for weekly ATS breakdowns" only if we actually ship that cadence). No "like if you agree".
- Length: typically 90-250 words. Dense is good; padded is banned.
- Formatting: short lines, one idea per paragraph, minimal bold. No emoji spam.

## Tone samples (source-derived)

Example voice (diagnosis tone):
"A CV is rejected for structure more often than for wording. The fix is not 'write better' — it is keyword coverage, section order, and date formatting. Here is what we change first when we review one."

Example voice (process tone):
"Job search is a funnel: saved, applied, interviewed, accepted. Most people track it in their head and the funnel breaks at follow-up. Agents keep the pipeline moving so you spend energy on interviews, not tabs."

Example voice (anti-hype tone):
"We do not promise interviews. We promise a better document, a better diagnosis, and a funnel that does not leak at step two. That is a real promise we can keep."

## Bilingual rule

- Content is written in the primary language of the platform audience (English for international LinkedIn) but may reference the French/Tunisian market explicitly when the claim depends on it.
- Never machine-translate between French and English; adapt the claim to the language's conventions.
