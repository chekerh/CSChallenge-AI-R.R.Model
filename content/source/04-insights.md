# UtopiaHire — Insights & Talking Points

Atomic, reusable ideas for public content. Each line is one claim, written to be expanded into a single LinkedIn post.

## ATS & recruiter reality

- Most ATS rejections are structural, not personal: keyword coverage, section order, and date formatting kill a CV before a human reads it. Fixing structure is a higher-leverage move than "improving English".
- A resume is not an autobiography; it is a filtered document built for a specific job description. The filter should be applied before you send it, not after you're rejected.
- "Tailor your CV" is useless advice without telling people *which parts* to change. Match scoring should name the gap.

## AI & hiring

- Using AI to rewrite a CV does not mean lying about experience. It means translating real experience into the vocabulary the job description and the ATS actually use.
- The value of AI in job search is not generating text; it is generating a diagnosis you can act on. A score without a reason is noise.
- Language matters: a French CV and an English CV are different artifacts with different conventions. Machine translation produces neither correctly.

## Job search process

- Job search is a funnel: saved → applied → interview → accepted. Most candidates track it in their head or a spreadsheet, and the funnel breaks at "forgot to follow up".
- A job search has agents, not just applications: keyword + location + schedule automation keeps the pipeline full while you sleep.
- Three tone switches (conservative, strong, premium) are enough to adapt one experience bullet to ten different applications without starting over.

## Tunisia & emerging markets

- Tunisian candidates applying internationally face a double tax: a weaker local job market and a format gap (French/English, ATS conventions). Both are fixable with the right workflow.
- Priced in TND, not USD: local SaaS that respects the local economy instead of importing US prices.
- Bilingual is not a checkbox. It is a real capability that changes how a CV is structured, not just which dictionary it uses.

## Craft & tools

- A good CV diagnosis should read like a code review: specific, prioritized, and actionable — not a letter grade.
- Automation should never remove the human from decisions; it should remove the grunt work (fetching, formatting, tracking) so the human has energy for the decisions.

## Anti-positions (what we do NOT believe)

- We do not believe in engagement-bait posts or fake "I'm hiring" content to farm comments.
- We do not believe a tool can get you a job. It can get you a better funnel, a better document, and a clearer diagnosis.
- We do not believe generic one-page CVs are the answer. Fit matters more than format.
