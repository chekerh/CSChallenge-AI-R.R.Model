# User personas

## Persona A — “Stage / First job” (student or new grad)

- **Goal**: get interviews quickly; CV that looks professional in FR/EN.
- **Frustrations**: doesn’t know what matters; CV feels “empty”; fear of making mistakes.
- **Motivations**: first credible job; internship requirements; social proof.
- **What they pay for**: step-by-step plan + wording that sounds senior without lying; templates + export pack.

## Persona B — Call center / support (high-volume applicants)

- **Goal**: pass screening; show customer-facing skills; demonstrate KPI awareness.
- **Frustrations**: generic CV; no guidance on what metrics are safe to include.
- **Motivations**: stable income; quick hiring cycles.
- **What they pay for**: role-specific rewrite packs; metric suggestions with “truth-safe” examples; fast tailoring per job.

## Persona C — Junior software/data applying internationally

- **Goal**: translate Tunisian education/projects to international expectations.
- **Frustrations**: unclear project descriptions; too much tech list, too little impact; EN tone.
- **Motivations**: remote/international opportunities; higher salary.
- **What they pay for**: bilingual pack; “recruiter lens” feedback; job match + gap analysis.

## Persona D — Career changer

- **Goal**: reposition transferable skills; show credibility fast.
- **Frustrations**: past experience doesn’t map; CV story incoherent.
- **Motivations**: major life upgrade; confidence and clarity.
- **What they pay for**: structured narrative rewrite; skill mapping; tailored cover letter drafts.

## Internal personas (operators)

### Admin (super admin)
- Manage plans, pricing content, feature flags, support content, and operational settings.
- Needs audit logging and safe publishing workflows.

### Support agent
- Resolve user issues, view account state, reset plan, see recent errors (read-only + limited actions).
