# Premium value — why users pay and return

## Why pay (vs free tools)

Free “CV scanners” often produce generic advice or fake ATS scores. UtopiaHire’s paid value is:

- **Explainable diagnosis**: users get *ranked actions* with clear “why” and examples.
- **Role targeting**: output shifts based on the role (internship, call center, software, etc.).
- **Bilingual coaching**: FR-first output, plus EN pack and bilingual guidance (Tunisia reality).
- **Honesty layer**: flags suggestions vs facts to reduce fear of “AI lying”.
- **Workflow, not novelty**: versions, exports, and (planned) job tracker tie into actual applying.

## “Worth paying for” bar (deliverables)

Paid users should consistently receive:

- A **short action plan** (top 5 fixes)
- A **score breakdown** users can trust (dimensions + evidence)
- At least one **high-quality rewrite pack** (3 tones) that is recruiter-usable
- **Job match**: gaps, keywords, and bullet rewrites aligned to a JD
- **Export pack**: ATS-friendly DOCX + PDF + naming conventions

## Retention loops (why users come back)

- **Job tracker**: each job has a tailored CV version + cover letter draft
- “Re-run audit” after edits with a **progress delta**
- Weekly digest: “3 improvements that move your response rate”
- Campaigns: graduation/rentrée job seasons (admin-controlled banners)

## Differentiation (Tunisia-first)

- Education phrasing guidance (Licence/Mastère/Ingénieur, FR/EN)
- Sector presets (call center/support, admin/finance, junior software/data)
- Cultural copy: professional FR/EN tone, not literal translation

## What we will not sell (trust)

- “Guaranteed interview” claims
- Fake ATS numbers with no evidence
- Auto-generated experience/metrics
